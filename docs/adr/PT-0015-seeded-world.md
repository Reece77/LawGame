# ADR PT-0015: The world after history: seeded cycles to 2034, dated shocks, a layoff action

- Status: **Accepted** (PT-M10 session, 2026-11-22; engineering decisions under CLAUDE.md §11, within ADR PT-0002 §2 and PT-M10's brief)
- Context: ADR PT-0002 §2 (history pinned to December 2025, then seeded), ADR PT-0014 (the macro history as dated indices), the PT-M10 brief's sub-tasks 0, 2-4

## Decision

1. **History to December 2025, then the save's own world.** `macro_history.yaml` (indices and the January rate rises) and `salary_scale.yaml` hold history to December 2025. `ptk.sim.macro_seed.world_data(data, balance, seed)` returns a save's `GameData` with those two extended to December 2034 from its master seed (memoised; nothing else in the data changes). The turn, player actions, org actions (in `ptk.db.org_io`), game creation, the balance runner and the API's views call it, so every reader of an index sees the save's world. A December 2025 point is always present, so no seeded value moves a month of history (golden: two seeds agree to December 2025 and diverge from January 2026).
2. **Each index is an AR(1) in logs, monthly,** fitted at load to its own July values 2005-2025 (`macro.seeded.fit_years`): the persistence and residual spread of the years outside history's recession episodes, the annual coefficient held in `phi_range`; a mean-reverting index reverts to its 2016-2025 mean, `growth` (G) to its fitted 2005-2025 trend. Stream `macro_seed` (65), key `(index_number,)` (its position in `macro_data.SERIES`), drawn once at turn 0.
3. **Recessions are regime switches with history's frequency and depths.** `macro.seeded.episodes` names history's two recessions (July 2008 to April 2009; February to April 2020). Each seeded month a recession starts with chance episodes / months fitted (2 in 252), at most one every `recession_gap_months`; it replays one episode chosen at random: over the episode's fall months each index takes that episode's log fall as impulses, and the AR(1) brings it back. Same stream, key `(len(SERIES),)`.
4. **Rate rises and the salary scale** (stream `scale_seed`, 66): the January rate rise is an AR(1) fitted to history's rises outside recession years, and takes history's mean recession-year rise in a year after a recession began (key `(0,)`). The scale steps in June when the past year's lateral heat is at least `scale.heat_from` and the year's rate rise (the profits proxy) at least `scale.rate_from`, two years after the last step, with chance `chance_bp`, by one of history's first-year steps since 2016 chosen at random; the year's bonuses move with it (key `(1,)`). The scale never falls.
5. **Dated shocks.** `macro_history.yaml` `shocks:` carries a shock the indices cannot (the 2020 pandemic: a counter-cyclical litigation index would otherwise rise): for its months, each named practice loses a share of its demand (`ptk.sim.macro.demand_factor`) and of its named matters' planned hours (`staffing.month_hours`: deals paused, hearings adjourned), and with `docket` open court cases' planned duration grows a month for each month (`progress.matter_month`), so a slowed court does not miss deadlines. Seeded recessions replay index falls only, never a dated shock.
6. **A layoff action** (`LayOff`, TST's `Fire`, PT-M1b's follow-up): an associate, counsel or member of staff (never a partner or a founder) works a notice month and goes (`end_reason` "fired", which the founder's persona already reads as a layoff); `pay.severance_months` of salary is paid at once; everyone remaining loses `morale.layoff_penalty`. It serves Reece's T4 pyramid script rule (2026-11-22); its screen is a follow-up.
7. Stream `macro_law` (32), reserved by ADR PT-0012 for "seeded legal indices after 2025", stays unused: the brief gives PT-M10 ids 65-69.

## Consequences

- Golden snapshots and the state cache were regenerated (CHANGELOG): history after July 2015 changed every save's indices from August 2015.
- From January 2026 every save is its own world; the same seed always gives the same one.
