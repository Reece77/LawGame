# Partner Track: real trial judges (research dossier, PT-M5 input)

Compiled 2026-11-20 for personal-use game data. Scope: trial judges of the eight modelled courts in `backend/data/courts.yaml` who were (a) in active service in January 2005, or (b) appointed 2005-2015. For D. Del., N.D. Cal., E.D. Tex. and W.D. Tex. it also includes judges already on senior status in January 2005 whose senior status began in 1990 or later (older senior judges are left out). S.D.N.Y. and E.D.N.Y. list active judges only, as instructed.

**Rules followed (ADR PT-0013):** no appointing president, party or ideology is recorded, and nothing about any judge favouring a side. Leaving is recorded only as kind and year: "senior (YYYY)", "elevated (YYYY)" or "left the bench (YYYY)". Wikipedia's "termination" reasons were used only to tell elevation apart from every other kind of leaving. No causes are given.

**Tags:** [S] sourced (URL in row), [D] derived (method stated), [A] assumed (basis stated).

**Year conventions [D]:** "joined" is the commission year in the Wikipedia judgeship table (taken from the FJC Biographical Directory). "Active" means still in active service at the end of the source tables (fetched 2026-11-20). For leaving years later than 2025, the row shows "active" (or only the senior year).

**Counts:** S.D.N.Y. 47, E.D.N.Y. 24, D. Del. 7, N.D. Cal. 29, E.D. Tex. 14, W.D. Tex. 17, Del. Chancery 9, N.Y. Commercial Division (Manhattan) 17. **Total 164.**

## Federal district courts

Per-judge procedural statistics were not found for any federal judge. Every judge inherits the court's figures in the statistics section, unless the row cites a published order or rule. Most per-judge notes are [A]: they record published opinions, rules or orders that are well known but were not fetched this session.

## Weaknesses

1. **Commercial Division roster is approximate.** 11 of 17 rows carry [A] dates. The official nycourts.gov pages (current justices, "former consolidated rules justices", history) and the Wayback Machine could not be fetched. The 2005 roster is solid only for Cahn, Gammerman, Ramos, Fried, Lowe, Moskowitz and Freedman. Other Manhattan justices may be missing for 2005-2015 (for example, justices who sat briefly).
2. **Federal dates come from Wikipedia's judgeship tables, not FJC pages fetched one by one.** Those tables are compiled from the FJC directory and are generally accurate, but no row was checked against fjc.gov. Years only; exact commission dates were not recorded.
3. **No per-judge procedural statistics.** Per-judge notes are mostly [A] (well-known published opinions, rules or docket reputation). Lex Machina, Docket Navigator or PwC patent studies would give per-judge time to trial (for example, E.D. Tex. Ward, Davis and Folsom, or D. Del. judges), but they are paywalled or were not fetched. Most judges should take court-level figures plus seeded noise.
4. **Senior judges' 2005 caseloads were not verified.** Senior judges in the non-NY courts are included on the [A] assumption that they still sat. Senior judges whose senior status began before 1990 (for example, D. Del. Schwartz, E.D. Tex. Steger, N.D. Cal. Conti and S.M. Williams) were left out.
5. **Duty stations** come from Wikipedia for current judges [S] and from memory for some former E.D. Tex. and W.D. Tex. judges [A].
6. **Elevations to state supreme or appellate courts** (Strine, Montgomery-Reeves, Moskowitz, Kapnick, Scarpulla) are [D] or [A]. The source tables give only the year the judge left the trial court.
7. **Outside the window but notable:** Alan Albright (W.D. Tex., Waco, 2018; large patent docket from 2019) and Colm Connolly (D. Del., 2018) are not included because they were appointed after 2015. The engine's "new judges are generated" rule covers them.
8. **Mauskopf (E.D.N.Y.):** the table records her as leaving in 2024. She served as Director of the Administrative Office of the US Courts from 2021, so her last year of trial work may be 2021 [A].
9. **The 2015 statistics PDF could not be fetched** (uscourts.gov returned 503 for that path). Only 2005 and 2010 figures are given.
