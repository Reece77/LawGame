# ADR PT-0017: Claims, insurance and discipline; the real-people rule's enforcement

- Status: **Proposed** (PT-M15, 2026-11-25; for Reece's and the product manager's approval)
- Date: 2026-11-25
- Builds on: PT-0003 §7 (client money), PT-0005 (hours), PT-0010 (bar rules), PT-0012 (streams 140-149), PT-0013 (real people); TST formulas.md §48 (visible stages; settle or contest; fines to 6970)
- Numbers and sources: `docs/research/risk-and-ethics.md`; keys: `risk.*` in balance.yaml, `insurance.yaml`, `discipline` in bar_rules.yaml

## Context

Spec §11.3-§11.6 makes mistakes and misconduct cost something. PT-M3a already records escaped errors on named matters; PT-M3b already has guideline rejections, padding and disputes; PT-M11 charged London a flat insurance cost and PT-M7 a per-lawyer insurance overhead. Nothing turned those into claims, premiums, sanctions or fines.

## Decision

1. **Scope.** Risk runs for the player's firm only, in phase 6 after payroll (`ptk.sim.risk.phase`). Rival firms' risk stays inside their pinned economics (PT-0008). `risk.enabled: false` switches the whole system off.
2. **Claims** (`ptk.sim.risk.claims`). Only two sources, read from state: the month's rise in a matter's `errors_escaped` (each escape a draw with chance `from_error_bp` x the GC's term x lax supervision x conflict checks off) and a lost case with stakes of at least `grievance_min_stakes_cents` (`grievance_bp`). A claim is made **latent** with a delay, a hidden merit (Beta) and size (log-uniform on the stakes or the expected fees) and the claimant's demand; it surfaces as a **notice**, then **investigation** (a drop chance on weak merit), **defence** (filed: public above `public_min_cents`, reputation in its practice; offers to settle), **trial** (lost with chance = merit, the award = the size). The firm answers an offer (`RespondClaim`); unanswered, the default rule settles when the offer is at most the firm's reading (the merit range's middle x the demand). The merit range is 2 x width wide, holding the truth at a drawn place, narrowed by the GC.
3. **Insurance** (`ptk.sim.risk.insurance`). Claims-made, per-claim limit and deductible, defence within limits. A payment splits exactly into deductible + insurer + excess (`formulas.split_payment`); the firm's part posts to `6310_claims`; when its cash cannot cover it, the equity partners pay in capital for the gap (by points; PT-0003 §5), mirrored in the founder's personal book. A policy year renews from the firm's first month: premium = max(insurer's minimum, max(trailing fees, lawyers x floor) x the market's dated rate x practice mix x experience modifier x insurer, limit and deductible factors). The modifier reads claims noticed per 100 lawyer-years and losses over premiums for `history_years`. A mutual takes firms of 50+ lawyers with a modifier at most 1.4. England and Wales offices raise the limit to the minimum terms at once (an endorsement re-pricing the months left). The premium **replaces** PT-M7's per-lawyer insurance overhead and PT-M11's flat England and Wales cost (both are skipped while risk is on).
4. **Discipline** (`ptk.sim.risk.discipline`). Cases against lawyers: triggered (a padder found, a surfaced conflict's lead, a noticed claim's lead for failure to supervise, a partner's client-money misuse) and drawn monthly (a complaint, misleading the court for litigators); chance = cause bp x exp(slope x (10 - integrity) / 10) x tags x lax x the GC's term. Hidden culpability from integrity; cooperate or contest (6600); at the hearing a finding with chance = culpability, the sanction by band: admonition, reprimand, suspension (on the books, no billable work: `interfaces.suspended`), disbarment (leaves; a partner is expelled through PT-M4's departure). Regulators' inquiries into the firm (`inquiries`) where its systems failed (a shortfall, three cases in a year, a conflict with checks off), by jurisdictions whose rules sanction firms: the SRA fines (bar_rules.yaml's cap), New York censures; settle or contest, fines to `6970_fines`.
5. **Trust accounts** (`trust`). A monthly reconciliation of each firm's client money. A shortfall (a posting error, or a partner's misuse) is recorded in the client book (`C1000` against `C1900_shortfall`) and made good from firm cash the same month (`6320_trust_shortfalls`; the client book's matching entry), so the bank equals what is owed at every month end (PT-0003 §7's invariant holds). It queues the critical `trust_shortfall` event, opens a case for a misuse and may bring an inquiry.
6. **Billing** (`ptk.sim.billing.audits`, `ptk.sim.risk.arbitration`). From 2006 large clients audit new bills (a cut by share, padding found); disputes are likelier on overruns and guideline rejections; a dispute the firm held and won may go to New York's Part 137 fee arbitration ($1,000-$50,000) and, above `public_min_cents`, is a public fee suit.
7. **The general counsel** (spec 9.1): the seat effect e lowers claims' conversion, discipline chances and posting errors, narrows the merit range, and drives a monthly billing review that finds padders. A vacant seat (e = -0.5) raises them. **The risk area** (spec 9.4) keeps checks on, answers offers by the default rule (settling above the deductible left is a one-off), settles inquiries within a guardrail, keeps the renewal's deductible within a guardrail.
8. **Firm policy.** `SetSupervision(lax)` frees `risk.policy.lax_hours` of each partner's non-billable hours a month and raises claims, discipline and misuse: the corner a firm can cut.
9. **The real-people rule (PT-0013).** Every draw that names a person skips a person flagged `real`: a claim's blame (`blame`), a case's subject (`open_case` refuses), misuse, the billing review. Tests assert it over generated and 30-year runs.
10. **Hidden values.** A claim's merit and size, a latent claim, a case's culpability, chances and draws never reach the API or a prompt; the API shows demands, stages, the merit as a range, money paid and outcomes.

## Consequences

- New accounts 6310 and 6320; schema v19 (`risk`, one JSON row, chained after PT-M13's v18).
- The golden snapshots and state cache change (premiums replace the overhead; claims and audits move cash).
- PT-M13's merged firm inherits the other side's open claims through `RiskState.claims` keyed by firm (the hook it leaves); PT-M12's funding and contingency read nothing here.
