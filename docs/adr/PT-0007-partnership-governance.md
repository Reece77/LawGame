# ADR PT-0007: The partnership governs: confidence, votes and elections

- Status: **Accepted** (Reece, 2026-11-17)
- Date: 2026-11-17
- Replaces for Partner Track: TST §14.6 (the board) and ADR-0018 §4's founder_removed game over
- Reuses: TST §14.6's confidence update, §25.6 (Chair and CEO), §45.5 (succession)

## Decision

1. **Partner confidence.** Each equity partner holds a confidence 0–100 in the managing partner (MP), updated monthly by TST §14.6's rule `B ← B + 0.2 (T − B)`, with the target from their own PEP against their expectation (a market benchmark for their practice and seniority), the firm's PEP trend, how the comp process treated them, their relationship with the MP (TST §16.7), and the MP's persona. Shown as a whip count (supportive, neutral, restless) with ranges, never true values.
2. **Votes.** Partnership decisions need a vote of equity partners, weighted by a rule in the partnership agreement (one partner one vote, or by points): admission of partners (December), de-equitisation, the partnership agreement's terms, mergers (two thirds by default), opening a region, a capital call, the MP election. Each partner votes by a utility on the proposal's effect on their PEP, practice, and their confidence; the player sees a predicted range and can lobby individuals (energy, promises, points).
3. **The MP election.** The founding partner is MP by the founding agreement. From stage 2 the agreement sets a term (default 4 years). At a term's end, or when average confidence is below 30 for 6 months (a "no confidence" petition), an election runs: the player stands; partners may nominate a rival candidate. **Losing is not game over:** the player chooses to stay as a partner (play continues, limited to their own practice and votes until re-elected) or to **hand over** (TST §45.5: pick a successor and play them). Ironman makes losing the election final.
4. **Game over** only when the firm **dissolves** (partners vote to dissolve, or insolvency under TST §14.7 with the revolver called) or the player leaves without a successor.

## Consequences

- The governor is the player's own people, so the game's central political skill is keeping rainmakers content: Football Manager's dressing room, in partnership form.
