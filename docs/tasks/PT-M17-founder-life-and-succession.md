# PT-M17: The founding partner's life, family and succession

**Goal:** the founding partner has a life outside the firm: a persona that the firm's choices shape, a personal account with draws, a capital loan, investments and tax, influence in the profession, a family, and an end. At retirement, an election loss or by choice, the player hands the firm to an elected successor and plays them, while the founder becomes senior partner and then a retired partner with an annuity.

**Spec:** §2.2–§2.5, §8.4 (retired-partner annuities), §8.6 (mandatory retirement in the agreement) · **ADRs:** PT-0003 (the personal book is separate; account 2600), PT-0007 §3–§4, PT-0013 (no private-life events for real people) · **TST to port:** formulas.md §32 (stress is PT's strain; persona §32.4; personal book §32.2; foundation §32.3; influence §32.5; legacy §32.6), §45 (family, children, education, estate, adviser, heirs, succession); `tst/sim/founder/` and `tst/sim/family/` · **Depends on:** PT-M7 (`pt-m7-done`) · **Lane:** C (beside PT-M7b in lane A and PT-M8 in lane B)

## Lane

- **Owns:** `ptk/sim/founder/` and `ptk/sim/family/` (new packages), personal accounts (codes beginning 9, the founder's book, kept apart by the ledger's existing rule), account 2600, the Founder and Family screens, `founder_life.*`, `family.*`, `succession.*` keys, RNG ids **50–59**.
- **Must not edit:** `ptk/sim/partnership/` (PT-M7b may tune it), `ptk/sim/org/` (PT-M8), `ptk/sim/billing/`, `ptk/sim/macro.py`.
- **How you reach the partnership:** read the founder's draws, true-up and allocation from the partnership's state (they exist from PT-M4) and post the mirrored personal entries in your own phase-7 step; run the succession election and the retirement-age vote through PT-M4's vote and election functions, exposed in `ptk/sim/interfaces.py` (add thin wrappers there, not changes inside `partnership/`). If a needed function is private, add a public wrapper in `interfaces.py` and record it in "Decisions made".
- **Shared files:** keep both sides; re-chain migrations if another merged first.

## Out of scope

Dynasty mode (v3), the founder's judicial appointment and government service (spec §19 ideas), pro bono's Beloved trait effect beyond the persona rule below, press (PT-M16).

## Sub-tasks (commit after each)

1. **The personal book** (TST §32.2): personal cash, investments, the capital loan (the founder's capital funded by a bank loan in their own book, ADR PT-0003 §5) and tax at `personal.tax_rate` on the founder's profit share; draws and the true-up arrive as mirrored personal entries. Property test: no 9-account ever appears in a firm entry; the personal book balances.
2. **Persona** (TST §32.4): Visionary, Ruthless, Beloved, Reckless, raised by firm choices (spec §2.3's list: de-equitisations, hostile laterals and layoffs; pro bono, raises and comp fairness; new practices; guarantees, revolver leverage, broken public claims). Effects as TST's, on partner confidence and recruiting.
3. **Foundation, influence and legacy** (TST §32.3, §32.5, §32.6): the foundation with an access-to-justice cause; influence as bar politics (the bar association, committees); legacy score from the firm, the name on the door, the foundation and the family.
4. **Family** (TST §45.1–§45.3, §45.6): partner, children, education, estate tax, the family office and the adviser. Life events apply only to the player's own generated family, never to a person flagged real (ADR PT-0013 test).
5. **Mandatory retirement and annuities** (spec §2.5, §8.6): the agreement's `retirement_age` (default 65, changeable by vote through the interface); from 60 the partnership expects a succession plan (a confidence term through `interfaces.confidence_change`); retired partners' annuities as the agreement grants (default none; if granted, a share of the retiree's last allocation for 10 years), booked to 2600 and paid monthly.
6. **Succession** (spec §2.5; TST §45.5): the player names a successor among equity partners (a child who made partner is eligible); the partnership elects them (PT-M4's election through the interface; a weak candidate can lose to a rival nominee); the player then plays the successor as MP: the save's player person changes, energy and persona reset to the successor's, the old founder becomes senior partner (an adviser with opinions, TST §45.5) until retirement. Triggers: by choice, at mandatory retirement, after a lost MP election (ADR PT-0007 §3), on health grounds (TST §45.5). Ironman: a lost election ends the game.
7. **Screens:** Founder (persona, personal account, influence, legacy, foundation) and Family; the succession flow (choose a successor, see the predicted vote range, confirm); e2e: retire the founder at 65 with a successor elected, and play on as the successor.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The personal book balances every month and never mixes with the firm's (property tests); the founder's after-tax income equals their draws and true-up less tax (reconciliation test).
- [ ] Persona traits move with the listed choices and their effects match TST §32.4's worked examples in PT units.
- [ ] A succession hands play to the successor with no change to the firm's state other than the MP seat, the player flag and the founder's status (state-diff test); replay is identical.
- [ ] No family or life event ever selects a person flagged real (test); no hidden value reaches the API or a prompt (test).
- [ ] Annuities post to 2600 and are paid from cash; the balance sheet balances with them.
- [ ] `make check`, `make e2e` and the nightly balance run green, or any changed target recorded with its before and after values; `Milestone-Tag: pt-m17-done`.

## Decisions made

Engineering decisions the spec and ADRs left open (the session's, 2026-11-21):

1. **Lane C reaches the partnership only through `ptk/sim/interfaces.py`.** Nothing in `ptk/sim/partnership/` changed. Thin wrappers added there: `partner_account` (a partner's or leaver's sub-ledgers), `equity_partner_ids`, `partner_ids`, `managing_partner`, `set_managing_partner`, `clear_election_result`, `ousted_founder`, `retirement_age`, `hand_over` (PT-M4's `actions.hand_over`), `open_partnership_vote` (`governance.open_vote`), `open_vote_kinds`, `election_utility` (`governance.base_utility`), `election_noise` (`governance.vote_noise`, still stream `vote`'s one call site), `vote_weight`, `vote_threshold_bp`, `record_vote`, `withdraw_vote`, `rival_nominee` (`election.rival_candidate`), `candidate_rating` (`governance.candidate_quality`), `predict_k`, `retire_partner` (the flight hook's `depart`, reason `retired`), `partnership_month` (what moves the persona), `partner_confidence` (engine-only), `terms_bias`, `terms_threshold_bp`, `partnership_votes`; and, for hiring, `founder_recruiting_term` and `founder_resign_mult`.
2. **The personal book** keeps each player's running balances in the state (`PersonalBook`) and its entries in their own tables (`personal_entry`, `personal_line`), like the client-money book; a property test holds them equal. Actions' entries wait in `FounderLifeState.pending` (never in the JSON) and the next turn carries them. The book opens in the first phase 7 a person plays (so saves from before PT-M17 get one), with $500,000 of savings and the founder's capital in the firm funded in full by a capital loan (ADR PT-0003 5); instalments are loan-funded too; the loan costs 6% and is repaid at 1/120 a month as cash allows (interest is added to the loan when cash is short); returned capital repays it.
3. **Tax on the profit share as it is paid out** (draws, the true-up, a leaver's current account): 45% (`founder_life.personal.tax_rate`: US federal top rate with New York State and City), paid with each receipt as estimated tax. Taxing December's allocation instead left founders owing tax on profit the firm never distributed (current accounts unpaid for years in the scripted runs); over a partner's career the two are the same. After-tax income = draws + true-up − tax (the reconciliation test). Annuity income is not taxed yet (follow-up).
4. **Persona sources in PT terms** (spec 2.3): de-equitisations (a passed vote, +10), hostile laterals (a partner joining from another firm, +3), layoffs (10%+ of the firm fired in a month; no layoff action exists yet), pro bono (December, the firm's moving average at 4 hours a lawyer a month), raises (half the staff in a month), comp fairness (December, no equity partner unhappy with their share), new practices (a practice group in a practice the firm never had), guarantees (a lateral's or a counter-offer's), revolver leverage (a month drawn above half the facility, at most 12 a year), covenant breaches; `first_region`, `early_ai` and `broken_claim` have their points but no source until v2's offices, AI eras and PT-M16's press. Sources count only while the player is the MP.
5. **Effects in PT units** at 60 (TST 32.4's values): Visionary +0.03 on every offer's score; Beloved resignations ×0.9 and +0.2 partner confidence a month; Ruthless −0.4 and Reckless −0.4 partner confidence a month (through `interfaces.confidence_change`). Hiring reads them through two interface terms (`hiring/phases.py`: the offer's pitch and the resignation factor). PT-M4's lateral partners' acceptance is unchanged (it lives in `partnership/`).
6. **The foundation's causes**: access to justice (added), education, health, local communities; climate dropped (TST's consumer brand has no Partner Track meaning). Effects for 12 months after a year's grants: education and local raise offers' scores (up to 0.03 at $10M of grants); access to justice and health lower resignations (×0.95).
7. **Bar politics** replaces regional lobbying: a committee seat (1.5 influence a month, 4 energy a month), spend on bar politics (account 6950, TST's `3 × sqrt(spend / $100k)`, up to $250,000 a month), 3% decay; from influence 40 the player may stand for bar president (June elections, certain from 70, two-year terms, +1 influence a month in office). Influence adds to recruiting (0.04 at 100) and partner confidence (0.2 a month at 100). No hidden scandal risk: the brief's bar politics carries none (a follow-up if wanted).
8. **Legacy** = 100 × log10(1 + the firm's best year's revenue / $10M) + 50 for the name on the door + TST's giving term + TST's persona term + 20 for each child who made partner + 30 for a successor from the family + 10 a year in bar office.
9. **The family** is generated with Partner Track's generator (a child as a future lawyer of a drawn practice; a partner as a business-services adult) and is never real (`real` is false; a real founder cannot have one and a real head runs none). A child's skills move toward their potential each month (`child_growth × e × closeness`), as Partner Track's growth formula needs a month of work; school at 12, law school at 18 (college folded in), qualified at 25, when a close enough child may join the firm as a first-year. Marriage follows `marriage_months` after meeting (TST's choice event, automatic here). Nepotism: when a child at the firm moves up a level, partners' confidence moves −2 if they rate below the median of their new level, +0.5 if above. The family stays with the founder: a successor plays without it (dynasty mode is v3).
10. **Estate tax**: the US federal history (rate and basic exclusion, IRS figures 2005-2026; 2010's repeal year at 0) on the head's personal estate above the exemption less lifetime gifts, charged at a succession on health grounds (TST 45.6); gifts to children (gift tax above the exemption, the unified US rule) and a family trust ($25,000 to set up; its assets are outside the estate) are the planning.
11. **Mandatory retirement applies to every partner of the player's firm**, not only the player (spec 2.5, 8.6): in phase 7 of the first month at or past the agreement's age a partner retires through PT-M4's departure (reason `retired`, no suitor: their clients pass to a colleague; capital and current account repaid over three years). The player's own retirement is a succession. With no other equity partner the agreement cannot retire its last partner: the player stays on until one is admitted (no game over). `tests/partnership_support.add_partner` keeps scripted test partners at 55 or younger so PT-M4's exodus script still tests its scenario (no assertion changed).
12. **Annuities** (spec 8.4, 8.6): the terms live in lane C's state (`annuity_terms`; default none, `founder_life.annuity`), because PT-M4's `Agreement` is in `partnership/`. When granted, a retiring equity partner's annuity is `share_bp` of their last December allocation a year for `years`; the whole (undiscounted) obligation is booked to 2600 against 3100 at retirement and paid monthly from cash as cash allows. The vote on annuities is counted inside the action (each partner's confidence, PT-M4's status-quo bias and how near they are to retiring, with the action's noise), since PT-M4's phase-1 count cannot apply a term its agreement does not have.
13. **The retirement-age vote** is PT-M4's terms vote, opened through `interfaces.open_partnership_vote` and counted in phase 1 like any terms vote.
14. **The succession election** is a PT-M4 vote of kind `election` with the parameter `succession` (PT-M4 never counts an open election vote: its elections run from the MP's term), opened by the action and counted by lane C in phase 10 of the same turn: each partner but the MP, the successor and PT-M4's rival nominee backs the successor when PT-M4's election utility plus `quality_weight × (rating − rival's rating) / quality_scale` plus noise is positive, at the agreement's election threshold; with no rival nominee the successor is unopposed. The predicted range uses PT-M4's band of indifference on the same utility.
15. **Losing a succession vote**: by choice, the founder stays MP and may name again after 12 months; when the founder must go (retirement, health) the partnership's rival becomes MP and is played; ironman ends the game (`election_lost`) then, as losing the MP election does. After a lost MP election (ADR PT-0007 3) naming a successor hands over without a vote (the elected MP keeps the seat); PT-M4's Election choice still works.
16. **The hand-over** changes only the MP seat, the player flag and the month's energy (PT-M4's `hand_over`), the old player's status and lane C's records (lives, books, the succession): the state-diff test holds this. The old player becomes senior partner (the adviser: opinions on each vote the firm opens; −2 confidence when a vote they opposed passes) and retires at the agreement's age, at once if already past it. A successor's persona resets to 20, or for the player's child starts at 20 + 25% of the parent's (TST 45.5); their book opens with $500,000 of savings and their capital's loan.
17. **Triggers**: from 60 the partnership expects a successor-designate (−0.5 confidence a month without one); at the retirement age the player has 3 months to name a successor, then the plan's (or the strongest partner as the firm rates them) is named; on health grounds each January from 70 (chance 0.1 × (age − 69), stream `family`; never a real person).
18. **Streams** (lane C's 50-59): `founder_life` 50 (a retiring partner's departure, a bar election), `family` 51 (members, life events, development, health), `investments` 52 (a player's returns). Schema v11 (`0011_founder_life`).
19. **API and screens**: `GET /founder`, `GET /family` and one POST per action; New Game takes the founder's age (34-52) and a family; a test-only route sets the founder's age (e2e). `GameOut.game_state` now admits `election_lost` (PT-M4's ironman result, which the API could not serialise before).
20. **Balance** (`make balance` on main and on this branch, seeds 1-10, 132 turns, the session's 2-core machine): no target changed its verdict (before → after): T3.lawyers 53.0 → 52.5, T3.revenue $63.87M → $70.64M, T4.steady $4.07M → $4.01M, T6.attrition 19.9% → 20.0%, T8.corporate -1.3% (n=32) → -0.8% (n=33), T10.ratio 0.88x → 0.87x, T12 274 ms → 263 ms (above 250 ms on this machine before and after; CI's runner is the gate), T6.partners 7.7% both (PT-M7's sampling-noise reading). Golden snapshots and the state cache are regenerated (CHANGELOG.md).
