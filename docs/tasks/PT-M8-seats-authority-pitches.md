# PT-M8: Seats, authority and pitches

**Goal:** the firm has a leadership. The player appoints people to the seats of spec §9.1 (executive committee, practice group leaders, office managing partners, the firm's general counsel, CFO, COO, CMO, CHRO, and later the pricing director and the chief knowledge officer); each seat's score moves the firm; seat holders get authority within guardrails and act each month; and they pitch big moves each quarter and report in their own voice.

## Sub-tasks (commit after each)

1. **Seat data** (`seats.yaml`): every seat of spec §9.1 with its stage, eligible levels (partner seats need an equity or income partner; business-services seats a business-services person at manager level or above, or a partner), the score weights and the effect. Practice group leader per active practice group (PT-M2's unit leader becomes the seat holder; migrate existing leaders).
2. **Seat score and effect** (TST §18.3): `s` = Σ weight × the holder's skill (true values, engine-only), `e = clamp((s − 10)/8, −0.5, 1)`, vacant from the seat's stage = −0.5. The API shows the holder's revealed ranges and a fit band, never `s`. Appoint, replace and vacate actions (energy; a partner seat holder's confidence moves by their ambition when appointed or removed).
3. **The executive committee** (spec §9.2): 3 members at stage 3, up to 9 at stage 5; appointed by the MP until the partnership agreement's `exco_elected` term is voted on (default appointed below 100 lawyers, elected at the December vote from stage 3). Its average confidence is reported quarterly as the partners' meeting summary (TST §14.6's warnings, through PT-M6's events).
4. **Effects in your own files and through the interface:** MP (confidence +0.5 e a month, through `interfaces.confidence_change`), executive committee (decision quality of firm-wide proposals), practice group leader (staffing quality and associate morale in the group), general counsel (conflicts-check quality: PT-M3a's hidden-conflict chance × (1 − 0.4 e)), CMO (RFP pitch quality +), CHRO (associate morale and campus quality); CFO, pricing and COO after `pt-m7b-done` (lane note).
5. **Authority** (TST §24): per seat, what the holder may do without asking, inside guardrails set by the player (hire within a plan, staff within a group, write down within a band, pitch for RFPs within a price band). Holders act in phase 2 with their own energy and the stream `seat_act` (45), keyed by `(seat_index, firm_id)`; their decisions are logged and recomputed on replay (ADR-0015 test). The spec §9.4 one-offs are never within authority.
6. **Pitches** (TST §27.1): each quarter, seat holders with authority pitch at most one big move (chance by ambition, quality by seat score; stream `pitch` (46)): a lateral partner with a book (PGL), a new practice group (PGL), a campus class size change (CHRO), a rate move for a practice (pricing), a panel push on named clients (CMO), a fit-out or lease (COO), a cost programme (CFO). Each has a low and a high case, Approve, Modify or Decline, and a follow-through record (TST §27.1) that moves the holder's morale and confidence.
7. **Reports with a voice** (TST §27.2): quarterly, each chief seat holder's short report (MockLLMAdapter in tests; templates offline; the holder's persona and tags flavour it; no hidden values in the prompt).
8. **Screens:** the Org screen's Leadership panel (seats, holders, fit band, vacancies with a candidates list, authority guardrails), pitches and reports in the Inbox; e2e: appoint a CFO, receive and approve a pitch.

## Decisions made

Recorded by the PT-M8 session (2026-11-21). Engineering choices and the gaps the spec, ADRs and brief left open.

1. **Seat stages.** Each seat has one stage in `seats.yaml`, from which it can be filled, its effect counts and a vacancy counts e = -0.5; before it a seat has no effect, filled or not (TST 18.3's "before stage 3, e = 0"). CFO, COO, CMO, CHRO and general counsel open at stage 2 (spec 3's "first business-services chiefs"; the brief lists the CHRO beside the CFO and its manual check appoints both in `pt-2008`, a stage-2 firm, so the brief's early CHRO wins over spec 3's stage-4 CHRO); the executive committee, practice group leaders, office managing partners and the pricing director at stage 3; the CKO at stage 4; the MP's seat at stage 2 (the partnership in full), so the early game is unchanged and the golden snapshots are untouched.
2. **The practice group leader's seat is PT-M2's unit leader** (`org_unit.leader_id`), not a copy, so the two never disagree and "migrating existing leaders" needs no data (migration `0011_leadership` adds only the `leadership` table). Appointing a PGL moves them into the group and makes them its leader through PT-M2's actions. A leader who is not an equity partner leaves the seat counting vacant from stage 3 (the panel says so).
3. **Who may sit.** Partner seats: an equity partner (the general counsel also an income partner, "a partner or counsel" read as partners). Business-services seats: a business-services person at `seats.bs_min_level` (manager) or above, or any partner. A person holds one seat besides the executive committee; the MP holds only theirs.
4. **`seat_effect`'s signature** is `seat_effect(state, firm_id, seat_key, balance, data, *, unit_id=None)`: the seats' weights live in `GameData` and the curve in `Balance`. A group's leader needs `unit_id`; `practice_leader_effect(state, firm_id, practice, balance, data)` is the convenience for a practice.
5. **The executive committee's term** (`exco_elected`) lives in the seats' own state, not PT-M4's `Agreement` (`ptk/sim/partnership/` belonged to lane A). Proposing it (action `exco_term`, energy 4) puts it to the December vote, which passes when the equity partners' mean confidence in the MP is at least `seats.exco.term_pass_confidence` (50). Elected committees are the equity partners with the most lockstep points (then book) at the December vote. Its e is the members' mean with each missing member of `min_members` (3) counted vacant. Its effect on "decision quality of firm-wide proposals": `exco_quality` x e added to every pitch's decision quality.
6. **The partners' meeting** is an inbox item each quarter's last month from stage 3 (the committee behind you, uneasy, or restless with a warning below `warn_confidence`), not a PT-M6 event template: no event trigger reads the committee yet (follow-up).
7. **Effects wired now** (coefficients in `seats.effects`): MP +0.5 e confidence a month (through `interfaces.confidence_change`); PGL flow work in the group's practice x (1 + 0.12 e) at intake ("keeps the diary full", `matters/intake.py`) and +4 e morale target for the group's associates; general counsel: an unseen conflict's surfacing chance x (1 - 0.4 e) (`matters/progress.py`); CMO: +2 e attribute points on an RFP pitch's quality (`rivals/rfp.py`); CHRO: +2 e morale target for every associate and +1 e attribute points on campus candidates' role skills (`hiring/phases.py`). Morale terms go through `interfaces.manager_morale_term`. Staffing quality proper (`matters/staffing.py`) stayed untouched (lane A's file); the PGL's utilisation effect is the diary.
8. **Authority** rules are in `authority.py`'s header: PGL hiring plans above a busy line within a headcount cap, the GC's waivers (never adverse to a key client), the CFO's write-downs of aged WIP within a band, the CMO's RFP pitches, the CHRO's campus targets in the register months, the pricing director's January rise within a band. A holder has `authority.energy_per_month` (40) for its seat's actions; their own energy is restored afterwards. The stream `seat_act` (45) is keyed `(seat_index, firm_id, slot)`: the brief's `(seat_index, firm_id)` plus the slot, because practice group leaders share a seat index. The COO acts only after PT-M7b (lane note).
9. **One-offs** (spec 9.4): `authority.ONE_OFF_KINDS` lists the action kinds that carry them; `apply_authorised` refuses those and anything outside `AUTHORITY_KINDS`, and a waiver adverse to a key client.
10. **Pitches.** Kinds and what Approve does are in `pitches.py`'s header. The panel push names its clients; a rate move is the firm's card (PT-M7a has one card, not one per practice: follow-up); a lateral partner pitch opens PT-M4's lateral search as the MP (the guarantee stays a one-off at the offer) without spending the MP's energy. Pitches wait in the inbox, do not block Continue, and lapse at the holder's next quarterly draw; no Holiday auto-approval. Modify changes a class size or a rate rise and approves. Stream `pitch` (46) keyed `(firm_id, person_id)`.
11. **"No pitch outcome reaches the API"** is read as the engine's draws (whether a pitch was made, which candidate, the quality and chance): the follow-through record (hit or miss against the central case, from the firm's own books) is shown, as sub-task 6 and TST 27.1 ask. The schema test forbids fields naming a score, quality, chance, probability, ambition, mistake or a hidden value.
12. **Reports** are PT-M6 read-only texts (`seat_report`, `ref_id` the holder) written after the turn by the LLM within the budget or by `reports.report_text`; the voice is from the holder's revealed traits and revealed tags only.
13. **The practice save** opens at the stage its lawyers give (`ptk/practice.py`), as phase 10 would set it at the month's end, so `pt-2008`'s stage-2 seats can be filled at once (the manual check).
14. **Acceptance measurement.** The PGL and pitch runs (`tests/balance/test_pt_m8.py`, nightly) use the grown 110-lawyer (100 for pitches) firm with resignations and partner flight off (`tests.seats_support.steady`): the grown fixture sheds a fifth of its people a year on main as here (12 months from 110 lawyers: 88 on both), which empties the seats the comparison needs. The PGL comparison sets every group's leader alike and pools the groups, because a group already at capacity cannot show the diary effect and choosing the slack group would pick the answer. Readings: morale 7.2 points and utilisation 3.3 points better (median of seeds 1-10); pitches a quarter median 2.0 at `pitches.base` 0.35, raised to 0.45 (this milestone's key): median 2.5 (seeds 1-10; no holder pitched twice in a quarter).

15. **Balance scripts fill seats** (decision rule 2). With every seat left vacant from stage 2, `make balance` read T3.revenue $57.10M (FAIL; main $63.87M) and steady's lawyers 45.5 (main 53, a known miss). A sensible player fills an open seat, so every script but do-nothing now appoints the best displayed-fit candidate to each vacant seat each month (`ptk/balance/seats.py`, called from `report.play` and the state cache's runner; no game code). After: T3.revenue $61.22M (ok), T4.pyramid $1.03M (ok; main $1.80M was a known miss), T5.standard 10% (ok; main a known miss), T6.attrition 20.5%, T7 all ok, T11 ok. T6.partners reads 7.7% on main and here (rule 3: not this milestone's; PT-M7b runs next). T12 (printed only) 274 ms against main's 256 ms on this machine, both over 250; its gate `make check-timing` passes.
