# PT-M4: The partnership

**Goal:** the firm is a partnership. Partners contribute capital, draw and are trued up; profit is shared by the chosen comp system; PEP is real; partners' confidence in the managing partner rises and falls; December brings the partnership vote and up-or-out; lateral partners arrive with books and guarantees; partners leave, and a firm can spiral into dissolution.

**Spec:** §2.5 (MP election only), §5.5, §8 (except offices and mergers) · **ADRs:** PT-0003, PT-0007, TST 0018 §4 (game-over states, adapted) · **Streams:** `vote`, `lateral`, `partnership` (17), `poach` (19) · **Depends on:** PT-M3b · **Lane:** A

## Out of scope

Seats, the executive committee and automation (v2); mergers (v2); succession beyond the election's hand-over choice (v2's PT-M17 ports TST §45).

## Sub-tasks (commit after each)

1. **Capital and draws** (spec §8.4): capital accounts, admission funding, capital calls (vote), returns after departure; monthly draws; December allocation and the true-up; retained reserve.
2. **Revolver and covenants** (TST §20.3's pattern): sizing by WIP and receivables, covenants including partner-departure share, breach events; insolvency and dissolution (TST §14.7 adapted).
3. **Comp systems** (spec §8.5): lockstep, modified lockstep, eat what you kill, committee discretion, two-tier; the December comp round (an action screen); satisfaction against contribution and market value.
4. **PEP and KPIs** (spec §8.1–8.2): PEP, leverage, margin, RPL; Explain decomposition of PEP.
5. **Confidence and votes** (ADR PT-0007): monthly confidence, the whip count with ranges, the vote engine (utility, noise, lobbying), the partnership agreement as data with a vote to change terms.
6. **Up or out and the December vote** (spec §5.5): eligibility, sponsors, business cases, equity and income admissions, counsel, departures to in-house roles (alumni flag for v2).
7. **Lateral partners** (spec §5.7): books as ranges, diligence, conflicts import (ADR PT-0009 §4), guarantees (account 2700), portability realised over 12–24 months.
8. **Partner flight** (spec §8.7): the monthly chance, approaches from the static panel firms (rivals proper arrive in PT-M5 and reuse this), counter-offers, clients following, contagion.
9. **The MP election** (ADR PT-0007 §3): term, no-confidence petitions, the election, losing (stay as partner, or hand over to a chosen partner and play them), ironman.
10. **Screens:** Partnership (partners, points, comp, contribution, market band, confidence; the agreement; votes; comp season), Finance additions (capital accounts, draws, revolver); e2e: hold a December vote and admit a partner; survive a lateral departure.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Capital accounts, draws and true-up balance (property tests); spec §8.1's worked example reproduced exactly.
- [ ] Under eat what you kill, a top originator's comp is at least 2× the median partner's; under pure lockstep, at most the plateau ratio (printed).
- [ ] A scripted exodus (three partners from one group in 6 months, with guarantees outstanding and the revolver drawn) dissolves the firm on at least 3 of 10 seeds without intervention, and on at most 1 of 10 with the scripted response (balance target T9's mechanism).
- [ ] Losing an MP election never ends a standard game; dissolution does.
- [ ] No partner's portability, true vote utility or confidence number reaches the API (ranges and labels only).
- [ ] `make check` and e2e green; `Milestone-Tag: pt-m4-done`.

## Decisions made

Engineering and design details the spec and ADRs left open (PT-M4 session, 2026-11-20). Every number is in `balance.yaml` under `partnership:`.

1. **The allocation is the year's cash-basis profit**: the accrual profit (income less costs on the ledger) less the year's growth in working capital (WIP and receivables net of their provisions, less deferred fees). ADR PT-0003 §3 names both views; distributing accrual profit that had not been collected bankrupted otherwise profitable firms in testing (group lift, seed 10). PEP (`last_pep_cents`) uses the same figure; the snapshot's trailing PEP is accrual.
2. **No year-end close entry.** Income and cost accounts keep accumulating; each allocation remembers where the year began (`pnl_closed_*`, `wc_closed_cents`) and debits 3100 against 2700. The Finance balance sheet shows earlier years' profit, less what was allocated, as undistributed profit, so it balances against this year's profit.
3. **Draws**: `draw_share_bp` (70%) of the budget a month; a guaranteed partner draws their guarantee even while draws are suspended (it is a contract). Budgets are last year's allocation; each March, June and September they are cut (never raised) to base × trailing-12-month profit ÷ last year's profit. A new partner draws from the month after admission. The first year's budget is the founders' books × 30%.
4. **Leavers**: their draws this year are their share (no true-up for a leaver's last year; `allocated_ytd_cents` comes off December's pool); an overdrawn current account is set off against their capital; the rest is repaid over 36 months from their leaving month, current account first. A departing partner serves the agreement's notice (3 months).
5. **The revolver**: facility = the scenario's limit, reset each January to 25% of last year's revenue if larger; borrowing base 70% of receivables + 40% of WIP, but at least 50% of the facility (the partners' personal guarantees: without it a new firm with one guaranteed lateral could not survive its first collections gap); 6.5% a year. It draws when cash is below zero and repays from cash above one month's costs plus the true-up owed. Covenants (tested only while drawn): drawn ≤ 30% of trailing revenue; partners' capital ≥ 50% of drawn; departed equity partners' books in 12 months ≤ 20% of revenue. Three months in breach and the bank calls it: repay within three months; each month end in default after that counts toward dissolution. The CFO warns within 20% of a covenant (cooldown 3 months).
6. **Dissolution**: three consecutive month ends below zero after the revolver (TST 14.7), or two with at least half the equity partners (MP aside) restless: they vote to dissolve. A called revolver alone does not dissolve a firm that is paying it down.
7. **Comp**: each credit is normalised on its own before the weights (EWYK 0.50/0.35/0.15). Management credit: the MP 1.0, unit leaders 0.5. Origination is PT-M3a's yearly origination credit; working credit the partner's own hours at standard value. Modified lockstep: 90% by points, a 10% bonus pool by contribution (moved by the comp round's adjustment). Committee: 0.4 × origination share + 0.6 × contribution, the comp round moving a share by up to ±30%. Guarantees are floors and promised comp comes off the top, both funded by the other partners; weights are prorated by months as an equity partner. Lockstep points step at each December allocation (with the round's gate: −1 to +2 steps); founding partners start at the plateau (25).
8. **Satisfaction** = comp ÷ max(contribution share × pool × (1 + 0.04 × (ego − 10)), V_mkt) − 1, clamped −1..0.5; V_mkt = 45% of the partner's book (trailing origination, else their book; at least $500,000).
9. **Confidence target** (ADR PT-0007 §1): 55 + 20 × satisfaction + 10 × the firm's PEP run rate against last year's + 8 × (MP's leadership and communication − 10) ÷ 10 − 12 while draws are suspended − 10 while a covenant is breached or the revolver called − 4 per equity departure in 12 months; one-off nudges (4 a comp-round step, −10 fear at a de-equitisation, PT-M6's events). The relationship-with-the-MP term is 0 for now (see follow-ups). Shown as supportive (≥ 60), neutral, restless (< 40), each partner's band ±8 around a value rounded to 5.
10. **Votes**: utility = (confidence − 50) ÷ 50 + lobbying 0.6 + 0.25 per promised point + the proposal's term + N(0, 0.5). Admission: 1.5 × (rating × 10 − 125) ÷ 20 + (practice utilisation − 0.75) × 2 − 4 × new points ÷ total (income partners at 30%) + 1 for the sponsor (0.5 for the sponsor's practice). Comp-system change: 3 × the voter's relative change in comp at last year's pool; other terms −0.2 (the status quo). Capital call: −2 × their share ÷ their capital, +1 if the firm is short or in breach. De-equitisation: 1.5 × (median contribution − the subject's) ÷ median; the subject votes no. One partner one vote by default; the MP votes for their own proposals; a vote passes with at least its threshold of votes cast (a simple majority is 5,001 bp). The predicted range counts partners within ±1 utility of indifference either way.
11. **Eligibility** is class year ≥ the agreement's `track_years` (8; voteable 6-12; a senior associate counts as class 9), counsel, and income partners (for equity). "Not this year" defers up or out for that December. Counsel is the MP's decision (a December proposal, no vote) at the counsel band. Associates not admitted, made counsel or deferred leave within 1-12 months; 40% go in-house (recorded as the departure's destination; alumni are v2). Sponsors: from class year 6, the player names one, or in September the equity partner of their practice with the best mentoring is given; PT-M1b's prospects term now reads it.
12. **Lateral partners** come from the partnership's own search (a $25,000 retainer; candidates generated with `people_gen` under a closed recruiter `Search` at level equity_partner, so the stream keys stay ADR PT-0012's): 1-3 partners, books log-uniform $1M-$6M shown ±50% (±15% after $20,000 of diligence, which also names the book's clients and conflicts), asks 35% of book (+3% per month of notice), guarantees asked 0-3 years, open 3 months. Acceptance: logistic(0.5 + 6 × (offer ÷ ask − 1) + 0.8 × (years offered − asked)). On joining: realised portability = hidden ± N(0, 20%); the book's non-conflicted clients follow with that chance, each in a month within 12-24 months; a recruiter fee of 25% of first-year comp. Conflicted book clients are simply dropped (no waiver or ethical wall in v1).
13. **Flight**: approaches come from the static panel firms (a practice's known strengths weigh double), offering V_mkt × U(1.0, 1.3) with 0-2 years guaranteed; the firm has a month to counter. The partner stays with probability logistic(0.5 + 5 × (firm comp + counter − offer) ÷ offer + 0.15 × (loyalty − 10) + 0.03 × (confidence − 50) + 0.4 × guaranteed years difference + 0.5 for a seat). A counter's comp is a promise the next allocation honours; its years become a guarantee. Contagion counts the partner's home practice (not their org unit). The player's own partner never flies. Each portable client follows with the partner's portability, 0-9 months after they leave; the rest pass to the remaining partner with the best familiarity in the practice, losing 10 relationship points.
14. **Stages** come from lawyers on the books (1, 4, 21, 101, 401, 1,501) and never go back. The MP's term starts when the firm reaches stage 2. The rival candidate is the equity partner with the most ambition (at least 12), then leadership. While the player is not MP the MP-only actions are refused and the sitting MP takes no decisions of their own (default rules run); the player stands again at the term's end. Handing over moves the player to any equity partner they choose (`is_founder` moves; energy follows).
15. **Interface for PT-M5** (`ptk/sim/interfaces.py`, pushed first): `PartnerFlightHook` with `leave_chance_bp`, `market_value_cents` (engine-only), `approach`, `counter`, `resolve`, `depart`, `arrive`, `approaches`, `departures`; an approach's view carries a stored band (`stay_label`), never the probability.
16. **PT-M6 integration**: `interfaces.confidence_change` now nudges partners' confidence; the partnership queues PT-M6's engine events `covenant_breach` (first month of a breach), `no_confidence` (when a petition forms) and `partner_group_exit` (a second equity departure from a practice within six months), only outside PT-M6's critical gap so crises stay spaced.
17. **Test-only routes** (`PTK_DEBUG_ROUTES=1`, set only by the e2e web server; hidden from the schema) set up a strong December candidate, an approach and a departure, so the e2e outcomes do not hang on seed luck.
18. **The scripted exodus** (acceptance; T9's mechanism, `tests/unit/test_partnership_flight.py`): the group lift plus three corporate partners ($600,000 comp, six clients each) and two litigators with $2.5M three-year guarantees; after a year the firm finances a $1.8M fit-out on the revolver; the three corporate partners leave in months 0, 2 and 4. The response (the CFO's warning heeded): suspend draws, call $1M of capital, repay the revolver from cash above two months' costs. Readings: seeds 1-10 dissolve 4 of 10 without intervention and 0 of 10 with the response; seeds 11-20: 5 and 0; seeds 21-30: 1 and 0 (seed variance; the acceptance is seeds 1-10).
19. **PT-M6's cash rounding** (rule 3, found on clean main too): an event's cash effect under a cap of less than a cent rounded past the cap; fixed in `ptk/sim/events/formulas.py` (PT-M6 has merged, so its file is open to integration fixes).
20. Retirement, mandatory retirement and annuities (account 2600) are not built: the agreement carries `retirement_age` for v2's succession (PT-M17).
