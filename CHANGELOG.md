Spec 15's eight categories (client, matter, people, partnership, market, regulatory, press, technology), each with minor, major and critical templates: 50 templates in `data/events/*.yaml`, including the critical set (a key matter's trial, a key exit, a key client walking, and the engine-queued partner group letter, no-confidence petition, merger vote, covenant breach, trust-account shortfall, malpractice claim, AI sanction and press storm for later milestones' engines, `queue_engine_event`). Restricted templates and effects never select a person flagged real (ADR PT-0013), and the validator rejects drafts that try. Phase 1 answers last month's minor and major events with their first choice; phase 12 queues next month's events (new stream `events`, 6) and the month's read-only texts (headlines, matter briefings, pitch letters, partner memos, the year-end letter). A written critical event blocks Continue (409 `critical_event_open`) until answered. After each turn commits, the event service writes events and texts (Claude within the $10 a month budget, else templates; a draft rejected twice falls back to its template) and logs every call. Schema v7 (migration `0007_events`, chained after PT-M3b's `0006_billing`). API: events, choose, texts, LLM settings (offline mode) and meter; `blocking_event_id` on the game; CLI `ptk events`, `ptk choose`, `ptk advance --answer-first`. Screens: event cards in the inbox, a critical-decision modal that blocks Continue, legal news on the Overview, the story-text settings with the budget meter; e2e `m6.spec.ts` (and `e2e/helpers.ts`'s `continueMonth` in `m1b.spec.ts`).
